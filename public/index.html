Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before initiating your project with us.

Website banne mein kitna time lagta hai? ▼
Basic Starter websites 3 se 5 din mein live ho jaati hain. Business growth websites mein 7 se 10 din lagte hain, jabki custom e-commerce ya custom portal projects 2 se 3 hafte mein complete hote hain. Har milestone par aapko live demo link par progress dikhayi jaati hai.
Kya aap Amazon aur Flipkart account registration and KYC bhi sambhalte hain? ▼
Haan, bilkul! Agar aap bilkul naye seller hain to hum GST verification, Seller Central account creation, Flipkart Seller Hub KYC, Category Ungating aur Brand Registry sab handle karte hain. Uske baad catalog listing, A+ content aur ads chalana sab hamari team karti hai.
SEO ka result kab tak dikhta hai Google par? ▼
Technical fixes aur on-page audit ke baad pehle 30 din mein Google Search Console par impressions badhne lagte hain. Realistic taur par 60 se 90 din ke andar aapke target keywords Google ke Page 1 aur top 3 positions par rank hone lagte hain.
Kya website banne ke baad hum khud content ya projects update kar sakte hain? ▼
Haan! KioraTech website ke sath aapko ek dedicated Server Admin Panel (`/admin`) milta hai jahan se aap bina coding ke naye projects, portfolio items, catalog packages, aur inquiries khud add, edit ya delete kar sakte hain.
Payment terms kya rehte hain? ▼
Website projects ke liye standard term 50% advance project kickoff ke time aur 50% final deployment aur live testing ke baad hota hai. Monthly management services (Amazon, Flipkart, SEO, Social Media) monthly retainer basis par hoti hain.
